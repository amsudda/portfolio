The problem

Retail buyers in Colombo knew the name but not the product range. Organic posting was inconsistent, the page had no content system, and paid campaigns were boosting posts rather than selling packs. Product photography was five years old and inconsistent between SKUs.

The brief was straightforward: build a repeatable content engine and make the ad account pay for itself within two quarters.

What we did

01
Content system

Four recurring formats — brew guides, estate stories, retail stockist features and UGC reposts — planned a month ahead.

02
Full SKU reshoot

Idearigs Studios shot the entire loose-leaf range over two studio days, plus a pour sequence for motion assets.

03
Account restructure

Catalogue sales campaigns with broad targeting, a retargeting tier at 18% of budget, and creative rotated fortnightly.

04
Reporting cadence

Fortnightly performance review against sell-through data supplied by the client's distributor.

DELIVERED ASSETS

“We had worked with two agencies before. This is the first time the reporting matched what our distributor was seeing on the ground.”

MARKETING DIRECTOR — CEYLON LEAF TEA CO.
